🔬 Bioinformatics Researcher | Cancer Genomics | AI-Driven Mutation Modeling

I'm Mohamad H. Hoballa, a PhD holder in Bioinformatics with a Master's in Molecular Genetics. My expertise lies in developing computational tools for identifying functional mutations in cancer-related proteins. Notably, I've created PFMI3DSC and PhiDSC, which leverage 3D structural alignment and statistical modeling to predict mutational hotspots, integrating data from AlphaFold, COSMIC, BioMuta, and Foldseek/DALI.

I'm passionate about applying machine learning techniques, including scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch, to large-scale omics data. Currently, I'm eager to extend my research into the realm of cell-free DNA (cfDNA) analysis for early cancer detection using generative AI approaches.

Feel free to explore my repositories to see my work in action.
